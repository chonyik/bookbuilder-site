Trust tax projection

Projects the federal (Form 1041) and California (Form 541) income tax of a trust for the year, from the client's Excel file. It is a projection to help a preparer plan, not tax advice. The file is read in this tab and never leaves your browser.

Upload the client's workbook

Choose the completed trust workbook (.xlsx) with the tabs Trust_Info, Ledger, Broker_1099 and Chart_of_Accounts. Columns are matched by their header names.

Tax category for each account

Each ledger account needs a tax category. The page suggests one from the account number or name; change any that are wrong. An account with no category is left out of the projection, and a check will flag it.

    Payments from this account count as California estimates already paid this year.

    Questions for the rest of the year

    For each category choose how to project the full year. Annualize scales the year to date up to twelve months. Actual only adds nothing more. Manual adds the amount you enter for the rest of the year.
